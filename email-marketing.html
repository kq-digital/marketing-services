Common KQuestions
Do I need a big list for this to work?

No. A few hundred engaged subscribers can outsell a list of ten thousand cold ones. If your list is small, we grow it while the automations do the selling.

Which email platform do you work with?

Yours. Whatever you're already sending from, we work inside it. And if you don't have one yet, we'll recommend the best fit for your size and budget, with no markup from us.

How soon will I see results?

Your first automated flow goes live within the first month, and it starts earning from day one. Campaign results build from the first send. Full momentum usually takes about a quarter.

What does it cost?

It depends on your list size and how much you want us to run. Get in touch and we'll give you a straight quote after the free audit.
