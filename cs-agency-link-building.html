Editorial link building at agency scale

  • Role Link building & digital PR specialist
  • Engagement May 2024 – Aug 2025
  • Scope UK agency delivery team
  • Placements Editorial only
10–15Editorial links per month, sustained
~3Concurrent client campaigns
DR 36–89Range of earned placements
0Paid placements

The problem

Agency delivery is a different discipline from single-client SEO. The constraint is not one campaign but a sustained monthly output across clients who rotate in and out, in sectors that share nothing with each other — and the output cannot dip in the month a new client lands.

What I did

As part of a UK agency delivery team I ran link building and digital PR for around three concurrent campaigns at a time, rotating across travel, eCommerce, and finance. Pure editorial links only: no paid placements, at any point, for any client.

The result

A sustained target of 10 to 15 editorial links a month across the rotating clients, with placements ranging from DR 36 to DR 89. A sample of the placements I personally earned:
