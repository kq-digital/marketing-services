Social and short-form video, end to end

  • Role Content specialist
  • Engagement 2021 – present
  • Scope Reels, carousels, campaigns, owned brand
  • Production In-house
5Pieces shown
4Formats
3Organisations
End to endHook, edit, caption

The problem

Short-form social is usually split across three or four people — someone writes, someone shoots, someone cuts, someone captions — and the idea gets diluted at every handover. Small publishers and small brands rarely have that many people, and the work still has to land in the first two seconds.

What I did

Wrote and produced each piece end to end: hook, script, edit, caption, publish. For a Southeast Asian political and literary publication that meant translating critical editorial material into formats people actually scroll — vertical video and multi-slide carousels — without flattening the argument into a platitude.

Alongside it, digital campaign production around reading events and anthology launches, press and advocacy communications as volunteer work, and full social management for my own small business: strategy, calendar, photography direction, and copy.
