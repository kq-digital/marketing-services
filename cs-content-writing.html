The standard behind the work

How to check any of this

Content is easy to claim and hard to verify — anyone can say they write. So none of it is described in the abstract here: every piece is linked, live, and readable in full.

01 / PROOF

Linked at source

Every piece on these pages links straight to where it was published. Nothing is reproduced here as a screenshot or a summary you have to take on trust — click through and read the real thing.

02 / OWNERSHIP

Honest about bylines

The commercial pieces were written for an employer and are published under that company’s ownership, not a personal byline. They are summarised and linked, never claimed as independent work.

03 / METHOD

Keywords confirmed first

Topics come from keyword research and validation, search-intent analysis, and gap and cannibalisation checks against the live site — not from guessing what might do well.

04 / CRAFT

Newsroom habits

Fact-checking against primary sources, a house style that holds across writers, and a pre-publication checklist — editorial discipline carried over from journalism into commercial work.
