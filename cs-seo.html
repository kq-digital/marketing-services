Insider Tips for a Stress-Free LTO License Renewal

The longest piece in the set. A step-by-step service explainer aimed at drivers dreading the queue, written so the brand is the answer to a problem the reader already has.

Executive pieces, in someone else’s voice

Thought leadership published under a general manager’s name. The hard part is not the argument, it is sounding like the person whose name is on it — and being willing to concede the things they would concede.

990Words · GM’s piece

GWMI’s Vision for Meeting Global Workforce Demand

Written in the voice of a president and a general manager, built around their own phrase — “Building Great Partnerships in the World” — and broken into its parts.

03 · Email

Email that gets opened

Most marketing email is negligible by design — written to be ignored. The approach here is relatability first and conversion second, and the numbers moved accordingly. These are averages across all handled business units.

22.93%Average open rate — the higher end of the 2025 B2B range
5.58%Average click rate, up from 2.93% in 2024
18.47%Average click-through rate, against a 10.12% company average in 2024
1.9×Year-on-year lift on both click measures
How these are counted Averages across every business unit handled, for the 2025 period, compared against the same accounts’ 2024 figures rather than an industry benchmark. They are platform-reported campaign metrics, not modelled estimates.
What is and isn’t claimed here These pieces were written for clients and are published under those companies’ ownership, so they are described rather than linked — word counts and client names are given so the scope is checkable, but the URLs are not mine to publish. The email figures are the one set of performance numbers in this section, and they are averages across handled accounts rather than a single best campaign.
